FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Intel

What is Rayder?

Brook's creator home base for public posts, releases, social links, creative projects, and eventually member-only extras.

What stays free?

Transmissions, public channels, and public creator content. Memberships are intended to unlock extras, not hide ordinary updates.

How will memberships work?

The plan is PayPal subscriptions with server-side verification. Payments remain disabled until login, subscription checks, and protected content delivery are implemented and tested.

Can I cancel?

Once launched, members will manage and cancel subscriptions through the payment provider. The site must reflect cancellations and expired memberships correctly.

Is the members area live?

Not yet. A page or hidden link is not access control. Inner Circle will only launch once server-side authentication and membership checks are active.
